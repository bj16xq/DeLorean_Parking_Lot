Welcome to the DeLorean Parking Lot project page!

Have you ever had a creative idea that was dismissed too readily? Perhaps the idea wasn't feasible at that moment in time, or perhaps the individuals in power were biased and had a narrow perspective on the situation at hand.

While "divergent thinking" is often celebrated due to the benefits of innovation, authentic divergent thinking often still faces various obstacles due to bias. However, one solution is to use creativity itself to capture creative ideas. When educational, research, organizational, and community practices can creatively support the sharing of ideas, then not only can we overcome obstacles to innovation, but we can increase engagement and participation in those spaces, which is particularly beneficial to youth and neurodivergent individuals who often face greater obstacles therein.

The DeLorean Parking Lot (DPL) method refers to strategies that encourage democratic participation in education, research, organizational change, and community engagement by recognizing how ideas may be feasible in the future or when seen through multiple perspectives, rather than dismissing them as infeasible or irrelevant right away. This interdisciplinary model combines the psychology of creativity with topics such as youth participation studies and futurology.

The DPL model can be applied to education, research, organizational change, and community engagement.
